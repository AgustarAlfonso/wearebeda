Decision Guide:
Merge Records
When to use: Both entries represent the exact same person or company.
Outcome: Combines data into the primary record and sets the duplicate record to MERGED so it is not handled twice.
Update CRM Record
When to use: The CRM record already exists, but the incoming message contains an updated phone number or email address.
Outcome: Updates contact fields in the master CRM record without deactivating or deleting either record.
Keep Separate
When to use: The suggested match is incorrect and both entries are distinct entities.
Outcome: Rejects the match and keeps both records active independently.
